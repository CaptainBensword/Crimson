A LITTLE ABOUT THIS PLACE

Made under
the Talon name

Crimson started as a place to collect music, but that idea never quite fit. This version is broader — a home for digital experiments, interactive objects, small tools, and new projects as they happen

It will change over time. Some parts might turn into full projects, others might stay as little experiments. That is kind of the point
