About Crimson

Crimson is a catalog for music across different projects and formats. It brings recordings, sheet music, and related files together so each piece can be found in one place.

What's in the library

The collections are organized around sheet music, game music, podcast and video music, standalone compositions, and other work. A piece can include an audio player, a score, individual instrument parts, or downloadable files.

Catalog status

The library starts empty. Music appears here when its actual files and details have been added; there are no sample releases or placeholder tracks.
